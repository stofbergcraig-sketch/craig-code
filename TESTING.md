## Power-fantasy balance and five-minute playtests

- Both guns now have three times their former magazines, including capacity upgrades: 24/96 base, 48/192 fully upgraded. Reload duration, recoil and weapon-animation curves are unchanged. Existing upgraded saves start with their correct larger magazines.
- New saves start with 100,000 Shekkles. Existing saves receive one top-up to at least that amount; spending and balances above 100,000 are preserved. The grant awards no kills or lifetime earnings. Four additional code-level scenarios cover the grant/migration, genuine earnings, gun magazines and the higher real-entity spawn target/caps; all six test files pass.
- Two browser-controlled sessions each ran for at least five active wall-clock minutes. Equipment was purchased at the shop and the first turret activated with normal E/F input; movement, aiming, sprint, double jump, dash, attacks, reloads and traps used input handlers. Diagnostics observed positions/enemies/progress rather than granting kills, cash or health during these sessions. The first ended at 1,364 kills and 100 health. The recorded repeat lasted 302.217 active seconds: 997 genuine kills, 313 live goblins, 100 health and no JavaScript errors. Its report is written to `/tmp/mirebreak-five-minute-result.json` by `tests/playtest-five-minutes.mjs`.
- The forward-only repeat controller spent part of its route pressed against a Gravewood tree. A separate actual-keyboard regression reproduced that position and backed out by more than 3m, confirming this was obstacle contact rather than a player soft lock. This limits conclusions about route coverage: the run covered the courtyard and Gravewood, not a complete traversal of every district.
- The new browser regression preserves a legacy 42-kill save while topping up its old 123-Shekkle balance. It verifies 36/144-round magazines with two capacity ranks, real mouse firing and keyboard reloading, 20 actual close-range AI attack callbacks without health loss, spending persistence, pointer lock, the Low cap and escaping the tree contact. The existing main browser regression passes movement/combat/shop/traps/settings/save/reset and the 150,000-kill ending again. The targeted purchasing/rewards/cracks and terrain/HUD browser regressions also pass.
- A 19.2-second fixed-step actual-map simulation used 16 towers across all seven types and the actual Low setting: 200 live goblins, at most 8 pooled projectiles and 276 particles (300-particle cap), 237 genuine kills and all enemy transforms finite. CPU simulation averaged about 1.92ms per update. This is bounded simulation validation, not rendered FPS. Production build and whitespace checks pass; development diagnostics are absent from the production bundle.
- Next design priorities suggested by the sessions: tightly grouped horde events to reduce time chasing scattered survivors; better crowd routes around scenery; a recommended crowd-clearing purchase path for the new starting budget. These are recommendations, not features claimed by this update. Short mixed-weapon sessions do not validate the45–60-minute full-run estimate.
- These are Chromium software-WebGL sessions driven by automated controls and screenshot review, not a human Windows playthrough or a hardware frame-rate guarantee. Subjective feel/audio on the user's laptop, full-run balance and long-session performance still need human evaluation.
